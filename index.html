Gridlock

Monsters march from the red rift to your green crystal. Build towers to shoot them, and to wall them into the longest possible route.

  1. Pick a tower from the bar at the bottom.
  2. Tap an empty tile to build. Towers are walls: enemies walk around them, and you can never seal the path completely.
  3. Start the wave. Every enemy that reaches the crystal costs lives. Survive 15 waves.
