## English

**App name (30 max):** Gridlock: Maze Defense
**Subtitle / short description (App Store 30 / Play 80):**
- App Store subtitle: Build the maze. Hold the line.
- Play short description: Your towers are the walls. Wall the monsters into the longest road.

**Full description**

The sky has torn open and the Hollow are marching on the last crystals. You are the Gridwright:
every tower you build is also a wall, and the monsters always take the shortest road left.
So don't leave them a short road.

- A 12-level story campaign across four regions, from the Greenreach to the heart of the Rift
- Enemies that change how you build: flyers that ignore walls, healers, splitters and bosses
- Six towers with three upgrades each, and four targeting modes
- Powers for when a wave gets out of hand, and early wave calls for bonus gold
- Earn up to three stars per level by keeping the crystal intact
- Classic mode: the original 15-wave challenge
- Works fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no in-app purchases
- English and Turkish

**Keywords (App Store, 100 max):** tower defense,maze,strategy,td,offline,puzzle,castle,defense,towers,crystal

**Category:** Games › Strategy · **Age rating:** Everyone / 9+ (mild fantasy violence)
